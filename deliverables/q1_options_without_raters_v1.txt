adol — Q1 options without human raters (2026-09-28; from the second brain: ~/repos/ideas/IDEA_LEDGER.md I-18..I-21)

Principle: without humans, validity must come from labels known BY CONSTRUCTION, from invariances that must hold, from groups known to differ, or from EXISTING human-labelled data. A stronger AI judge is not an anchor (rejected, I-21).

A (I-18, recommended core) Label-free validity battery + failure-aware aggregation.
   Planted violations: take real KYRA transcripts and insert one CF sentence at turn d, or delete the crisis resource, so the correct label is known. Measure judge sensitivity by depth and length.
   Metamorphic tests: paraphrase or padding must not change the verdict; the insertion must.
   Aggregation: compare the tie rule against invalid = missing.
   Claim: judge output failures rise with transcript length, and missing-output rules turn them into depth effects. The battery measures and removes this artefact without raters.
   Kill pilot: about 720 views, 3 judges, ~10 GPU-h, $0. KILL if planted-CF sensitivity is flat in depth and failures are flat in length, or if a length/keyword baseline matches the judges.
   Seeds: our 16/20 tie flags, the crossfam parser case (499/9,600 labels), N4. A new mechanism row was added to negative-results.md.
B (I-19, recommended second leg) Known-groups validity.
   The same base models are pushed by system prompts at 4 doses toward secrecy, exclusivity and isolation (relational groups only; no sexual content, no self-harm methods).
   The benchmark must order the doses monotonically.
   KILL if Spearman < 0.5 or a length baseline orders the doses as well.
C (I-20, conditional) Anchor on EXISTING human-labelled multi-turn safety datasets.
   Calibrate the panel there, then test the transfer to Korean scripts.
   Blocked on a data/license check; KILL if no usable corpus exists.
Rejected: a frontier model (Claude/Gemini) as the reference label. It is another judge, not validation.

Recommended paper: A + B in one Q1 manuscript ("measuring multi-turn youth-safety without raters"), with C added only if a corpus exists. It reuses the phase-A transcripts and judges; new compute ≈ 1–2 GPU-days; $0.

Copyright Transfer Agreement
Must be signed and returned to the editor-in-chief of the
journal before the manuscript can be considered for publication.
 

  The copyright transfer agreement from author(s) to the Korea Institute of Information & Communication Engineering (KIICE) must be clearly stated in writing to enable the KIICE to assure dissemination of the author(s)’s work. Therefore, the following agreement, executed and signed by the corresponding author, is required with each manuscript submission.
 
Manuscript Title:  ______________________________________________________________________________
Publication Title:  Journal of the Korea Institute of Information and Communication Engineering
 
  When the article is accepted for publication, I, as the corresponding author, hereby agree to transfer to KIICE all rights, including those pertaining to electronic forms and transmissions, under existing copyright laws.
 
- I agree the copies made under these circumstances will continue to carry the copyright notice that appeared in the original published work. 
- I agree to inform my co-authors, if any, of the above terms. 
- I certify that I have obtained written permission for the use of text, tables, and/or illustrations from any copyrighted source(s), and I agree to supply such written permission(s) to KIICE upon request.
- I agree that open access rights allow third-party users to copy, distribute and display the published version of the article, and create derivative works, subject to appropriate attribution and non-commercial exploitation.
- I agree the ‘RESEARCH AND PUBLICATION ETHICS’ of KIICE which is described in ‘Instructions for Authors’.

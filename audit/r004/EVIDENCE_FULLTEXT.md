## What this record establishes (conductor's reading)
1. Matched literal-vs-adapted evaluation, including Korean, is ESTABLISHED for general safety: Culturally-Adapted Red-Teaming (paired DT/CA, +9.3 pp), ROK-FORTRESS (transcreation matrix, Δling ≈ 10 pp), KSAFE-MM (translation vs contextualized). All single-turn, adult/general, ASR/TRS outcomes. → H3 is a domain-specific replication/extension (youth relational, multi-turn, human-anchored rubric), not a design novelty.
2. Human-anchored automated judging is standard (at least nine of the sixteen report judge/classifier-vs-human statistics, Persona-Grounded's classifier accuracies included; ▲r002). Statistics differ in metric, reference (consensus vs pairwise), unit and denominator — K-Bench's 94.2% (judge vs adjudicated consensus) vs 89.4% (clinician pairwise) are not the same comparison, so "judge–human ≥ human–human" is not by itself an acceptance gate. → method, never a claim; KYRA's judge gate needs a common reference (research/CRRI_SPEC_v1.md).
3. Turn-level dynamics exist in adjacent domains: TAF-MED (collapse 61.4% + recovery counts + transition matrices, medical, 3 fixed turns), CompanionBench (asymmetric gate retreat/rebuild, adult), TSJ (AULC, causal trace, ≤210 turns, no recovery), KIDBench (slope/peak drop, 7–11y). → A discrete-time hazard-with-recovery model in the youth relational domain is a representation choice; the substantive claim must be about information in relational history (H5), per F-r001-5.
4. CRRI's four axes have named cousins: CAREBench (Emotional Dependency: Social Isolation Reinforcement, Anti-Referral Pressure; Relationship Engineering: Luring & Target Isolation), INTIMA (Isolation, Retention), TSJ (Emotional Dependence domain), AICompanionBench (Control, Manipulation). Secrecy: ▲▲(r003) KIDBench SCORES secrecy — discouraging secrecy is evaluated within Boundary-setting (p.28) and encouraging secrecy is penalized in the Family/Relationships and Online-Safety rubric (p.31); Persona-Grounded has a "Secrecy" scenario theme (Tab.8). What no paper read has is a separately named secrecy-INDUCEMENT axis scored per turn; that dedicated-axis distinction is the only one KYRA may draw.
5. Not found in any of the 16 full texts: a CUMULATIVE multi-turn index whose predictive/incremental validity is tested against an independent practitioner global judgment on held-out scenarios/models; Korean-language adolescent (12–17) relational/crisis conversations; ITEM-PAIRED lexical benign twins of each escalation script (equal-turn benign dialogues themselves exist in KIDBench, Tab.17 — ▲r002). These remain proposals, not results.

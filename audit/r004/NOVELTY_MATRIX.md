## C. Pitch v2 (rewritten per F-r001-3/4/5/6; written after the r001 lock)
Empirical question (not a causal premise): In Korean-language conversations that simulate adolescents aged 12–17, does a history-sensitive relational-risk score (CRRI: late-turn-weighted sum of four practitioner-defined axes) predict blinded senior-practitioner global judgment of relational-safety failure BETTER than final-turn, maximum-turn, unweighted, refusal-only and INTIMA-CRB-count scores — on held-out scenarios, two held-out models and both age bands? (H5/H6; success bars in research/CRRI_SPEC_v1.md.) Three distinctions are kept explicit: unsafe model behavior; an evaluator missing it; disagreement with practitioners. ▲▲ Comparator set (r002/r003): the five baselines above PLUS turn count, a defined recency score (exponential weights λ = 0.5 over r_t) and a transcript-aware competitor (dialogue-level axis intensity: each axis rated once for the whole conversation, summed — the same rater information basis as CRRI). Inference is LIMITED to incremental criterion performance relative to these defined comparators: CRRI is itself a linearly recency-weighted mean, so a win does not by itself identify accumulation (versus a single severe late turn or conversation length) as its source; a claim that the gain measures accumulation would need a separate discriminating analysis that is not registered here. Weights and any tuning are frozen on development data; holdouts keep scenario families, twins and repeated runs together; the warranted claim is CONCURRENT criterion validity on the same transcript (not prospective early warning, and never developmental harm) unless a separate prospective test restricts inputs to pre-failure turns.
Within-session construct and window: "cumulative" = within one conversation of ≤8 turns (early-relationship window); failure = first turn with D2 = 0 or D6 = 0 or a listed critical failure; recovery is reported in three defined forms (▲r002): immediate (restored at t+1 after the FIRST failure), eventual (restored at any later turn), sustained (restored and no relapse through the window); end-of-window censoring is stated (a final-turn failure has unobserved recovery); any-turn failure remains true after recovery (TAF-MED's separate denominators). Turn-level events and transition counts are primary; a discrete-time hazard model is one representation. No long-run, lifetime or developmental-trajectory claim (TSJ's regime).
Localization, narrowed to a paired comparison (F-r001-4): treatment = a fixed rewriting procedure (은어·학교·또래 맥락·국내 위기자원 substitution) applied to an item's literal Korean translation, holding risk content, turn count, explicitness and rater/judge language constant; outcome = RB-6D dimension scores, critical-failure rate and CRRI as judged by human-anchored raters; estimand = within-item paired difference. This replicates the DT-vs-CA effect established for Korean general safety (Culturally-Adapted Red-Teaming +9.1 pp; ROK-FORTRESS; KSAFE-MM) in a new domain and unit of analysis; it is not an isolated "effect of culture".
Endpoints separated (F-r001-6, ▲F-r002-1): model over-refusal (denominator = genuinely benign REQUESTS; reference = benign-request label) and judge over-flagging (denominator = independently established benign RESPONSES/transcripts — a harmful answer to a benign prompt flagged by the judge is a true positive) — two numbers, two reference labels. Controls: equal-turn benign dialogues already exist (KIDBench Tab.17); what KYRA proposes beyond that is an ITEM-PAIRED lexical benign twin for each escalation script, matched on turn count and, as far as feasible, token length and informational demand — a proposal to be implemented and verified at S2, not a verified property. Judge calibration (Korean adolescent dialogues) is a SEPARATE gate from CRRI criterion validity: common reference or leave-one-rater-out comparison, critical-error limits, uncertainty, coverage by language/age/risk, held-out assessment (PI decision 6).
Residual contribution, classified honestly: (1) a Korean-adolescent relational/crisis benchmark with matched controls = benchmark EXTENSION; (2) the H5/H6 measurement result = the candidate IMPORTANT claim, currently a proposal; (3) the paired localization comparison = domain replication of an established effect; (4) hazard + recovery = analysis representation. Whether (2) is enough to lead a Q1 paper is the PI's decision (VERDICT r001 item 1).

**F-r001-1 — MAJOR — Central novelty exclusions are unsupported by the supplied evidence.**

Every fixed comparator has stronger method, population, or absence claims than its excerpt establishes. This affects the central defense that predecessors lack criterion validation, recovery, or a matching design. R:12's sweep-wide “NOT found” and R:18's claim that none of the modules supplies the new measurement claim cannot be upgraded to demonstrated literature gaps from this package. Precise numerical claims are also builder-only here; metadata verification does not verify them.

Disposition: **Open; qualification required.** Replace unsupported negatives with “not established in the supplied excerpt,” or substantiate them with permitted paper-level evidence in a later review. The present safe statement is that these excerpts do not establish the exact proposed conjunction. This finding prevents novelty clearance but does not demonstrate that a prior work already contains the core.

**F-r001-2 — MAJOR — The localization novelty defense omits directly relevant corpus comparators.**

Evidence: M:18 and M:26; R:12(c); C:63, **Culturally-Adapted Red-Teaming Across East and Southeast Asian Contexts** (2606.09178); C:81, **ROK-FORTRESS: Measuring the Effect of Geopolitical Transcreation for National Security and Public Safety** (2605.14152). Their titles and excerpt openings place comparative cultural adaptation and translation-based safety evaluation directly in the relevant space. CultureConverse (C:32, 2608.28405), already mentioned in the lock's final paragraph, adds culturally grounded multi-turn evaluation. LoCar (C:78, 2605.21086) is a further localization/control lead in a different application domain. These are absent from the builder's differentiation rows.

Disposition: **Open; comparator reconciliation required.** Compare the exact intervention, pairing, controls, population, and claimed inference before asserting localization-design novelty. The corpus does not reveal whether any of these works uses the proposed matched causal design, and ROK-FORTRESS's title alone does not establish Korean-adolescent coverage. This finding establishes an incomplete comparison, not a confirmed priority defeat. It does not change the fixed closest-three selection.

**F-r001-3 — MAJOR — The opening “fail because” claim confuses measurement validity with a demonstrated cause of failure.**

Evidence: M:26 says evaluations fail under cumulative escalation **because** their proxies are unvalidated and attributes this explanation to the practitioner study. C:10 stops before providing the study's findings. TSJ and TAF-MED (C:57, C:41) establish relevant topics, not that deficient criterion validation causes evaluation failure. Even the builder's own summaries describe detection of multi-turn degradation using the criticized measures (M:7, 10, 13).

Disposition: **Open; substantive wording correction required.** State an empirical question: whether a history-sensitive index predicts an independent practitioner assessment better than specified alternatives. Distinguish unsafe model behavior, an evaluator missing that behavior, and disagreement with practitioners. A qualitative critique would motivate the question; it would not by itself establish the proposed causal explanation or show that a new index repairs model safety.

**F-r001-4 — MAJOR — Pair matching and an English anchor do not yet specify the claimed causal effect.**

Evidence: M:26 and R:12(c) promise isolation of the causal effect of cultural localization. These files do not define the treatment contrast, target outcome, assignment/evaluation procedure, or which item properties are preserved. This is a limitation of the supplied design description, not evidence that the eventual design cannot identify an effect.

Disposition: **Open; narrow or specify the claim before treating it as the main novelty.** Define whether the intended effect is on model behavior, judge scores, or measurement validity. State what literal and localized variants hold constant and what changes; address risk severity, conversational structure, and evaluator-language effects. A controlled effect of a particular rewriting procedure can be a legitimate target, but it is narrower than an isolated effect of “culture.” An English anchor alone does not resolve these distinctions. Until specified, describe a proposed paired localization comparison rather than an established causal measurement contribution.

**F-r001-5 — MAJOR — The residual measurement contribution is a testable proposal, with unresolved construct and time scope.**

Evidence: M:7 limits the project to early dynamics within eight turns; M:26 leads with a cumulative relational index and survival with recovery; R:18 calls validated CRRI plus boundary-survival the new measurement claim; R:27 warns against long-run accumulation. TSJ explicitly occupies time-accumulating developmental companion risk (C:57), while Persona-Grounded and CogManip occupy multi-turn companion/manipulation evaluation (C:85 and C:66, 2606.06099). Measuring what Matters establishes construct validity as an existing benchmark concern (C:15).

Disposition: **Open; PI must choose the substantive claim and success conditions.** Define “cumulative” as an explicit within-session construct and retain the early-window limit throughout; eight turns cannot support a long-run or lifetime interpretation. Define failure and recovery events and the observation window: a first-failure time alone does not specify recovery after that failure. Distinguish a new empirical result about relational history from applying a hazard analysis or naming an aggregate CRRI. M:26's held-out comparisons against final-turn, maximum, unweighted, refusal-only, and CRB baselines are a useful proposed discriminator, not a result. Separate criterion raters (R:26) reduce one stated concern, but agreement with practitioner judgment remains a defined criterion rather than observed developmental harm. No completed experiment is required at S1; the issue is the claim the eventual experiment would warrant.

**F-r001-6 — MINOR — Judge over-flagging is not generator over-refusal, and the control design is still pending.**

Mark turn matching as a proposed S2 requirement, not a verified implemented property. Equal turn counts address part of the duration concern; whether the chosen controls isolate relational content is a design question, not settled novelty evidence.

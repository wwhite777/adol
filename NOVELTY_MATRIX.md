## Pitch (written AFTER the auditor lock, 2026-09-16)
Current safety evaluations of conversational AI for minors FAIL UNDER cumulative relational escalation BECAUSE their outcome measures are unvalidated proxies — refusal rates and judge scores with no independent criterion (Cha et al.'s practitioners say exactly this; KIDBench/TSJ/TAF-MED measure multi-turn degradation but validate no index against human judgment). KYRA-Bench + CRRI ADDRESS THIS by (1) validating a cumulative relational-risk index against blinded senior-practitioner global judgment (reliability, G-study, held-out incremental validity vs final-turn/max/unweighted/refusal-only/CRB baselines), (2) modeling boundary failure as a discrete-time survival process WITH recovery, (3) isolating the causal effect of cultural localization via matched literal/localized item pairs with an English anchor, and (4) jointly measuring safety and over-refusal on lexically AND turn-matched benign controls — ACROSS Korean adolescents 12–17 (two age bands), 6 risk domains, 5+2 models, T1/T2 time points.
What we do NOT claim (forbidden-wording input for S2): first youth/child benchmark; first simulated-minor multi-turn evaluation (KIDBench/ChildSafe/TSJ); first clinician/practitioner-anchored judge (VERA-MH/K-Bench); first Korean-localized safety benchmark (KSAFE-MM); first evidence that first-turn safety is an incomplete proxy (TAF-MED); long-term/lifetime risk accumulation (TSJ's ~140-turn regime vs our ≤8).
